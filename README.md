# Data_324_FinalProject

This is the Start of Data 324 Final project where we will be looking at interactive graphs and how they are made, along with various features that they will show. 

Main research question

How do income, credit score, and requested loan amount relate to loan approval decisions?

Supporting questions
1. Can a higher income compensate for a lower credit score when comparing loan approval patterns?
2. Do applicants requesting larger loans have lower approval rates, even among high-income applicants?
3. Do approved and rejected applicants have different credit-score distributions across income, gender, and age groups?
